# Roboto source

Velora uses the normal Roboto family, weights 400, 500 and 700, from the
official Roboto Classic v3.016 distribution:

- Distribution: <https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-3-classic/releases/tag/v3.016>
- Files: `hinted/static/Roboto-Regular.ttf`, `Roboto-Medium.ttf` and `Roboto-Bold.ttf`
- Official family metadata: <https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/roboto>
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, copied to `OFL.txt` from the official Google Fonts repository.

The three local files were checked for the required Ukrainian characters
`Ґґ Єє Іі Її ₴`, the Ukrainian apostrophe `’` and digits before use.
