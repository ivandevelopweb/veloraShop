# Velora store context

This file is the server-side source of truth for general Velora information.
It is intentionally kept outside the client bundle and should be updated when
store policies or contact details become final.

## Store

Velora is a Ukrainian online store with prices in Ukrainian hryvnias. The
catalogue focuses on fragrance, care, home goods, accessories, wellness,
technology and gifts. The current catalogue is a test catalogue, so product
information must always come from the database supplied to the assistant.

## Delivery

The checkout currently offers Nova Poshta delivery to a branch or parcel
locker, and Velora courier delivery within Kyiv. Delivery is free for orders
from 1500 UAH; otherwise the delivery fee is 90 UAH.

## Payment

Checkout uses the configured LiqPay flow. The assistant must not claim that a
payment was made and must not perform checkout or payment actions.

## Returns and support

Return rules and support contact details are placeholders until the store
owner publishes final policy text. If a customer asks for a detail that is not
present here, say that the information is not currently specified and direct
them to the store contact page rather than inventing a policy or contact.

## Store history

Velora is a Ukrainian storefront prototype. No additional public history is
currently configured.
